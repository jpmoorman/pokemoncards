Scanner speed test

This measures how fast card scanning runs on this phone, so the numbers can be sent back and compared. It takes about a minute or two.

It opens the camera once, only to read its resolution: no picture is taken or sent. The sample cards are public card pictures from TCGdex (drawn ones if those can't be downloaded). Nothing you saved in the app is changed.

Open the app itself once first (and reload it if it offers an update), so this page measures the latest version. For first-time numbers, reload this page just before tapping Run. Keep the screen on and this page open until it says it is done.
